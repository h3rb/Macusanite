Macusanite 0.1.13. The navigator folds into an edge tab, groups wear captions on the map, and the canvas gets its own menu. Signed Windows and Linux installers with updater support.

## Highlights
- The canvas **navigator** now closes into a **drawer** on the canvas's right edge instead of disappearing: a slim vertical tab, sized to its own contents rather than the whole height, slides the minimap back out, and **Pop out** in the drawer returns it to a floating panel.
- **Group names become captions** at map scales. From the semantic handoff (below 55%) a group's title is centred over the top edge of its box at a compact card's label size — the same words, still readable — while the corner tab that shrinks to a speck steps aside.
- **Right-click empty canvas** for the canvas's own menu: new note, a new annotation at that point, select all cards, fit every card, back to 100%, Overview, and undo and redo named by the step they would take. Cards, groups, wires and annotations keep their own menus.
- The **vault sidebar folds** to a vertical rail on the left edge so the centre pane takes its width, and clicking the rail unfolds it again; the choice is remembered per vault.

Linux users can choose the AppImage or Debian package. Both are signed, and the updater manifest includes Windows and Linux.
