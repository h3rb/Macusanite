Macusanite 0.1.15. The canvas edits the outline itself, teaching notes remember being closed, and the navigator's groups stand out. Signed Windows and Linux installers with updater support.

## Highlights
- **Edit the outline from a card's menu.** **Insert note after** adds a sibling below the card's whole branch, **New child note** adds a first child, **Promote** and **Demote** move the card and everything under it one heading level, and **Move up** / **Move down** swap it with its previous or next sibling, branch and all. Positions, types, fields, bonds, groups and the canvas's own undo steps follow the card, so reordering two headings that share a title never leaves one card's data on the other.
- **Rename in place.** Press Enter or leave the title field and the new name is written; same-vein links that named the old heading, along with the bonds and bond notes around it, are rewritten with it.
- **Preset cards.** The toolbar's **+ Add ▾** palette offers a blank note or **Person**, **Place**, **Scene** and **Faction** cards seeded from the vault's presets. The markdown stays the source of truth.
- **Teaching notes remember being closed.** Close the object-map note, the search pane's quote tip or the editor's shortcut hint with its own button and it stays closed for the vault; **Settings → Reading → Reset tutorials** forgets those decisions and turns teaching notes back on, while the switch beside it hides them without forgetting anything. Notes closed before this release are remembered too.
- **Groups read on the navigator.** Each group is drawn in its own colour — a tint beneath the cards and an edge above them — roughly twice as thick and seven times the contrast of the old grey hairline, so a cluster is a region rather than an outline you have to hunt for.

Linux users can choose the AppImage or Debian package. Both are signed, and the updater manifest includes Windows and Linux.
